## 2. Meta Conversation-Based Pricing Model (Pakistan)

Meta charges per 24-hour conversation window based on the conversation category and the country code of the recipient (+92 for Pakistan).

### Meta Conversation Categories:
1. **Service Conversations (User-Initiated):**
   - Triggered when a patient messages the clinic (e.g., asking for timings, doctor availability, booking, or FAQs).
   - Once the clinic/bot responds, a 24-hour customer service window opens.
   - All subsequent incoming and outgoing bot messages within this 24-hour window are covered under this single conversation charge.
   - *Rate for Pakistan (+92):* ~$0.0195 USD (approx. PKR 5.45) per conversation.
   - *Note on Meta Free Tier:* Meta provides **1,000 free Service conversations per WABA per month**.
2. **Utility Conversations (Business-Initiated via Pre-Approved Template):**
   - Triggered by the clinic to send critical transactional updates outside or inside the 24-hour window:
     - Appointment confirmation notices
     - 24-hour and 3-hour appointment reminders with reply-to-cancel buttons
   - *Rate for Pakistan (+92):* ~$0.0150 USD (approx. PKR 4.20) per conversation.
3. **Authentication & Marketing Conversations:**
   - Marketing (promotions, seasonal aesthetic packages): ~$0.0280 USD (PKR 7.85). *Not used for clinical booking bot unless explicitly authorized by Dr. Samiya.*
